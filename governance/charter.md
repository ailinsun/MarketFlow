# Experimental research methodology

MarketFlow is an independent research and engineering project. AI tools assist with analysis, coding and review; the maintainer remains responsible for public claims and corrections.

## Evidence

- Define the research question and sampling window before interpreting a result.
- Separate observations, model calculations and hypotheses.
- Link each quantitative claim to its available data or method.
- Preserve measured values when editing descriptions, attribution or formatting.
- State missing inputs, selection effects and limits to reproduction.

## Review

- Check accounting identities and the assumptions behind them.
- Test methods using local synthetic inputs where raw observations cannot be shared.
- Treat AI-generated annotations as provisional until independently reviewed.
- Describe uncertainty and counterexamples alongside conclusions.
- Record corrections explicitly; do not silently replace historical measurements.

## Publication

- Publish aggregate research material without participant identities or private infrastructure details.
- Use the public author name and current repository links consistently.
- Preserve dated snapshot filenames and document metadata changes.
- Provide licences, provenance, citation information and reproducible checks with each release.

The checks in this repository support review; passing them does not validate every scientific claim.
